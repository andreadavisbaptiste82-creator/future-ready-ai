Pattern Detectives

How Machines Notice Similarities — a public AI Discoverers student lesson for grades 3–5.

Classroom-Ready Resource

Patterns, classification, and human judgment

Students investigate how people and machines sort objects by noticing similarities. They compare sorting rules, discover how examples and labels influence a result, and explain why people must review important machine-made classifications.

Learning Outcomes

What students will learn

Notice and classify

Identify observable similarities and differences, state a sorting rule, and apply it consistently.

Compare and explain

Use evidence to explain a category and recognize that more than one reasonable rule may exist.

Check and reflect

Identify possible mistakes or unfair groupings and explain why human review is necessary.

Accessibility and differentiation

  • Directions may be read aloud and modeled.
  • Students may point, circle, draw, dictate, or use sentence frames.
  • Activities avoid relying on color as the only feature.
  • Tasks can be reduced or extended while preserving the reasoning goal.

Responsible AI safeguard

The lesson teaches a foundational AI concept without requiring student use of a generative AI system. Any optional demonstration should be teacher-led, use an approved tool, and avoid personal or sensitive information.
