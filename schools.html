Professional Learning

Professional Learning for Responsible AI Integration

Flexible sessions for educators, school leaders, instructional teams, technology leaders, and CTE programs — delivered as a 60–90 minute session, half day, full day, or ongoing series.

Core Offerings

AI Literacy Foundations for Educators

Build a shared understanding of how AI and generative AI work, and what responsible classroom use requires.

Responsible Use of Generative AI

Establish clear practices for privacy, transparency, disclosure, bias awareness, and human accountability.

Evaluating AI-Generated Content

Strengthen the ability to check accuracy, sources, and evidence in AI-generated claims and content.

Bias, Fairness, and Human Oversight

Examine how data and system design can create unequal outcomes, and where human review must remain central.

Advanced and Role-Based Offerings

Privacy, Data, and Digital Citizenship

Explore sensitive information, consent, and responsible participation in AI-enabled environments.

Academic Integrity and Assessment in the AI Era

Redesign expectations and assessments so student thinking stays visible alongside AI use.

AI Integration by Subject Area

Connect AI literacy to existing standards and authentic tasks within a selected subject or pathway.

AI Leadership and Readiness for School Teams

Support leadership teams in assessing readiness and planning responsible implementation.

Career and CTE AI Integration

Connect AI literacy to authentic career pathways and workplace expectations.

Train-the-Trainer and Instructional Coaching

Prepare internal leaders to facilitate learning, model lessons, and coach educators through implementation.

What Is Already Built

Curriculum architecture plus implementation infrastructure

The public website shows selected examples. The protected development system also includes teacher, student, assessment, family, administrator, LMS, and pilot-support resources.

AI Discoverers

Six-week Grades 3–5 core pathway with K–2 adaptation guidance, student activity resources, and the public Pattern Detectives sample.

AI Explorers

Eight-week Grades 6–8 responsible AI and digital systems pathway with portfolio evidence and implementation supports.

AI Innovators

Nine-week Grades 9–12 pathway spanning responsible use, verification, fairness, synthetic media, human-centered design, career connections, and capstone evidence.

Educator learning

Seven-session professional-learning architecture plus readiness, implementation, and action-planning tools.
