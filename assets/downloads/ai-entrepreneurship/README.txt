IMPLEMENTATION NOTE

This resource is designed for local review and adaptation. It is not described
as district-approved or standards-approved. Educators and school leaders should
confirm local curriculum, technology, privacy, accessibility, academic
integrity, student-research, and acceptable-use requirements before
implementation.

Students should not collect confidential or sensitive personal information or
deploy an unreviewed AI system. AI tools are optional; the module can be
completed without student access to generative AI.
