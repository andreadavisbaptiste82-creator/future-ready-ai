Accessibility Statement

Accessibility practices

Future Ready AI is designed to support educators, school leaders, students, and families using a wide range of devices and assistive technologies.

Current website practices

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and descriptive link text.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, accordions, filters, forms, and visible focus states.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and transcripts or text alternatives for featured videos.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow for smaller screens without requiring horizontal page scrolling.
  • Reduced-motion support for visitors who request less animation through their device settings.
  • High-contrast text and button states designed for readability on light and dark surfaces.

Future Ready AI does not make a formal accessibility-conformance claim until a dedicated accessibility review is completed. If you encounter a barrier, please use the contact page to identify the page, resource, or interaction that needs attention.
