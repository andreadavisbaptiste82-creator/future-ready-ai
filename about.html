Welcome to Future Ready AI.

Future Ready AI was created to help students and educators build the knowledge, confidence, and judgment needed to thrive in an AI-powered world.

We believe AI education should be age-appropriate, responsible, creative, and deeply human-centered. Students are not simply learning how to use new tools. They are learning how to think critically, ask better questions, evaluate information, protect their privacy, recognize bias, and make thoughtful decisions.

Our goal is to prepare learners and educators to engage with artificial intelligence with confidence, curiosity, and integrity.

At Future Ready AI, we are learning, creating, and leading together.

Mission

Why we exist

To help K-12 learners and educators develop the knowledge, judgment, creativity, and confidence needed to engage responsibly with artificial intelligence.

Vision

Where we're headed

A future in which every learner can understand, evaluate, and use AI as an informed creator and responsible digital citizen.

Our Commitment

Inclusive and ethical learning, by design

  • ✓Human judgment remains essential — AI supports thinking, it doesn't replace it.
  • ✓Privacy and safety come first in every lesson and tool recommendation.
  • ✓AI-generated information must be evaluated, not accepted at face value.
  • ✓Bias and fairness are examined openly, in age-appropriate ways.
  • ✓Creativity is supported, not replaced, by AI tools.
  • ✓Students learn to disclose appropriate AI use in their work.
  • ✓Every school receives clear, age-appropriate implementation guidance.

Founder

Learning and Development bridge: Andrea Davis-Baptiste’s experience connects curriculum, technology adoption, workforce capability, educator development, and career preparation.

Andrea Davis-Baptiste

Andrea Davis-Baptiste is an educator, learning and development leader, instructional technology specialist, and AI enablement strategist with more than 17 years of experience supporting students, educators, school leaders, and large-scale technology initiatives.

Her work brings together learning science, digital transformation, responsible artificial intelligence, leadership development, and practical classroom implementation. Throughout her career, she has designed and delivered learning experiences, supported technology adoption, coached educators, and helped learning communities build the confidence and judgment needed to use emerging technologies effectively.

Andrea created Future Ready AI to help schools move beyond simply providing access to AI tools. Her vision is to ensure that students learn how artificial intelligence works, how to question and evaluate its outputs, how to use it safely and ethically, and how to apply it to creativity, problem-solving, and future career pathways.

Guiding Principles

What Guides Our Work

Future Ready AI is grounded in the belief that artificial intelligence education should strengthen human thinking, creativity, responsibility, and opportunity. These principles guide the design of every curriculum, professional-learning experience, and school partnership.

Human Judgment First

AI should support learning and decision-making without replacing student thinking, educator expertise, or human responsibility.

Responsible by Design

Privacy, safety, transparency, ethical use, and age-appropriate expectations are embedded from the beginning.

Curiosity and Critical Thinking

Students learn to ask thoughtful questions, evaluate information, recognize limitations, and verify AI-generated content.

Equity and Inclusion

Learning experiences should be accessible, culturally responsive, and designed to expand opportunity for every student.

Learning Through Creation

Students build understanding through inquiry, collaboration, design, reflection, and authentic problem-solving.
