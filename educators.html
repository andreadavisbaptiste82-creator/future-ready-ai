Welcome, educators.

Future Ready AI is designed to support teachers, instructional leaders, and school teams as they bring artificial intelligence into learning in meaningful and responsible ways.

We know that educators need more than theory. You need practical strategies, classroom-ready resources, and guidance you can trust.

That's why we focus on helping educators understand AI tools, teach students how to use them responsibly, strengthen digital citizenship, and build learning experiences grounded in creativity, critical thinking, and human judgment.

Whether you are exploring AI for the first time or building a broader implementation plan, we're here to support you every step of the way.

Let's prepare learners for the future—together.

Professional development for educators

Seven sessions for responsible AI in K–12

Future Ready AI professional development helps educators and school leaders build the knowledge, confidence, and practical strategies needed to use artificial intelligence responsibly and effectively in K–12 environments.

Sessions may be delivered on-site or virtually and can be offered individually, bundled into half-day or full-day learning experiences, or incorporated into a broader school or district implementation plan.

0160 minutes

AI Literacy & Readiness

Recommended audience
K–12 teachers, instructional coaches, school leaders, and district teams
Participants leave with
A shared understanding of AI terminology, opportunities, limitations, and readiness considerations for schools
0260 minutes

Privacy & Student Safety

Recommended audience
K–12 teachers, school leaders, instructional technology staff, and district leaders
Participants leave with
A practical framework for evaluating AI tools through privacy, data protection, age-appropriateness, and student-safety considerations
0360 minutes

How Generative AI Works (and Fails)

Recommended audience
K–12 teachers, instructional coaches, school leaders, and support staff
Participants leave with
A working understanding of how generative AI produces responses, why errors occur, and how to recognize limitations such as hallucinations
0460 minutes

Prompting, Research & Verification

Recommended audience
K–12 teachers, instructional coaches, librarians/media specialists, and administrators
Participants leave with
A reusable prompting and verification process educators can apply to lesson planning, research, communication, and classroom activities
0560 minutes

Assessment Integrity & Academic Evidence

Recommended audience
Grades 6–12 teachers, instructional coaches, curriculum leaders, and school administrators
Participants leave with
Strategies for redesigning assessment tasks, clarifying acceptable AI use, and gathering stronger evidence of student learning
0660 minutes

Bias, Fairness & Human Oversight

Recommended audience
K–12 teachers, school leaders, instructional coaches, and district teams
Participants leave with
A practical checklist for recognizing bias, questioning AI outputs, and maintaining meaningful human oversight
0790 minutes

Classroom Implementation & Action Planning

Recommended audience
Teacher teams, instructional coaches, principals, district leaders, and implementation teams
Participants leave with
A school- or team-level AI implementation action plan with priorities, responsibilities, classroom expectations, and next steps

Recommended professional learning pathways

Choose the starting point that fits your team

Sessions 1–3Half day

AI Foundations

Designed for schools or districts beginning their AI journey.

Participants build a common foundation around AI literacy, privacy and student safety, and how generative AI works.

Approximate instructional time: 3 hours, plus breaks and discussion.

Sessions 4–6Half day

Responsible Classroom Practice

Designed for educators ready to move from AI awareness into responsible classroom practice.

Participants explore prompting and verification, assessment integrity, bias, fairness, and human oversight.

Approximate instructional time: 3 hours, plus breaks and discussion.

Session 790 minutes

AI Implementation Workshop

A facilitated planning session for school or district teams ready to translate professional learning into implementation.

The session can also be expanded into a longer planning workshop for leadership teams requiring additional policy, curriculum, or implementation support.

Length: 90 minutes

Sessions 1–7Multi-day

Full Professional Learning Series

Schools and districts may complete all seven sessions as a coordinated professional learning series delivered across multiple days, professional development days, leadership meetings, or scheduled learning cycles.

The sequence is intentionally designed to move teams through:

  1. Understand
  2. Evaluate
  3. Practice
  4. Verify
  5. Protect
  6. Implement

How engagements work

From first conversation to follow-up support

  1. 1

    Discovery Conversation

    We begin with a conversation with school or district leadership to understand priorities, current AI use, student and educator needs, existing policies, and desired outcomes.

  2. 2

    Readiness & Needs Review

    Together, we identify the audience, professional learning priorities, implementation considerations, and the appropriate starting point for your organization.

  3. 3

    Customized PD or Pilot Plan

    Maison Glamour et Grâce develops a recommended professional learning, pilot, or implementation plan aligned to the school’s or district’s goals. This may include individual sessions, a bundled professional development experience, leadership planning, educator cohorts, or a Future Ready AI pilot.

  4. 4

    Professional Learning & Implementation

    Sessions are delivered virtually or on-site with an emphasis on practical application, educator participation, responsible AI use, and resources participants can immediately apply.

  5. 5

    Follow-Up & Next-Step Support

    Following delivery, we help leadership teams identify next steps, implementation priorities, additional professional learning needs, and opportunities to expand or refine the program.

Typical timeline

Most professional development engagements can move from the initial discovery conversation to delivery within approximately 3–6 weeks, depending on district scheduling, customization requirements, and the scope of the engagement.

More comprehensive pilots or district-wide implementations may require additional planning time.
