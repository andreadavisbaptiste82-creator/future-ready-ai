Career-Connected Extension • Grades 6–12

What the extension covers

Drone Career Explorer connects AI literacy to systems thinking, aerial data, responsible technology, mission planning, industry analysis, and workforce readiness. The curriculum is designed to be completed through cases, research, simulation, conceptual planning, and locally supervised experiences.
